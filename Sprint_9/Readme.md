//DMart Sales Analytics Dashboard | Business Intelligence Project
Transforming raw retail sales data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and data visualization.


//🚀 Project Overview
The DMart Sales Analytics Dashboard is a Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Excel and Google Looker Studio to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends.
This dashboard converts complex sales data into visually appealing and interactive reports, enabling stakeholders to make informed business decisions based on real-time insights.


//🎯 Project Objectives:
✔ Analyze overall sales and profitability performance
✔ Identify top-performing product categories
✔ Track monthly sales growth and seasonal trends
✔ Evaluate region-wise business performance
✔ Understand customer payment preferences
✔ Generate actionable recommendations for business optimization
